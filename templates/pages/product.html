Paternity testing

A clear answer, from a laboratory that has to stand behind it.

A paternity test compares the child's DNA with the alleged father's. Half of a child's DNA comes from each biological parent, so the laboratory checks whether the tested man's DNA matches the half the child did not inherit from the mother. Including the mother's sample makes the comparison stronger, but the test can be done without it.

Decide which kind you need before you book

The laboratory analysis is the same. What differs is how the sample is collected and documented — and that cannot be fixed afterwards.

For your own knowledge

Sometimes called a peace-of-mind test. It answers the question for the people involved and nobody else. It is not prepared for use by a court, an embassy or any other authority.

  • Simpler identification requirements
  • Results shared only with the people named on the order
  • Usually the lower-cost option

For legal or immigration use

Needed for matters such as changing a name on a birth certificate, family court proceedings, visa and immigration applications, or inheritance disputes.

  • Collection witnessed and documented under chain-of-custody rules
  • Strict photo identification for every participant
  • Report issued by the laboratory in the form the authority requires

Whether a report is accepted is decided by the authority receiving it — the court, embassy or government department — not by us or by the laboratory. Tell us who the report is for before you book so the right process is followed from the start.

What happens after your appointment

The swab does not stay in Pakistan. Once it is sealed and documented at our Lahore office, it travels by tracked international courier to the laboratory handling your test.

Transit normally takes 3–5 working days. Laboratory processing time depends on the test and on whether all participants' samples arrived together; it is confirmed to you in writing when you order. We do not promise same-day or next-day results, because the sample has to cross a border first.

If your matter has a deadline — a visa interview, a court date — tell us the date when you book. We will tell you plainly whether it is achievable.
