Terms and Conditions

DNA Testing Solutions

What we are

DNA Testing Solutions is s logistics service based in Lahore, Pakistan. We are not a laboratory. We do not carry out DNA extraction, testing, genetic analysis or result preparation in Pakistan. Our Lahore office collects your sample, verifies it against your order, seals and documents it, and ships it by international courier to an accredited partner laboratory in the United Kingdom, the United States or Australia. That laboratory performs the testing and issues your report.

Eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to order or consent to a test. A parent or legal guardian may authorise testing for a minor or for an individual in their care.

Nature of Service

We coordinate the following, with the testing itself performed by an accredited partner laboratory outside Pakistan:

Legal and immigration DNA testing, arranged under the receiving authority's chain-of-custody requirements
Wellness and lifestyle testing (nutrition, sports and gut-microbiome analysis)
Ancestry and ethnic-origin testing

Our role is limited to case coordination, collection, verification, documentation, international shipment and customer support. We do not perform any laboratory testing or genetic analysis ourselves, and we do not diagnose or treat any condition.

Collection and Submission

Most tests use a painless buccal (cheek) swab
Samples must be collected and handled using the instructions provided. Improper collection or handling may lead to inconclusive or invalid results, and the partner laboratory may require a fresh sample.
We reserve the right to request a new sample where the laboratory's acceptance criteria are not met.

Confidentiality and Data Privacy

Your personal data and sample are shared only with the partner laboratory carrying out your test and the courier handling the shipment.
The genetic analysis and the report are produced by that laboratory under its own privacy and quality framework; your genetic data is processed by the laboratory, not by us.
We adhere to applicable Pakistani data protection law and handle your information solely to arrange and support the test you ordered.
We do not release results to anyone other than the people named on your order without your written instruction.

Use of Results

Results are prepared and issued by the partner laboratory that performed the testing. We do not write, interpret or alter them.
Reports are provided for informational purposes and are not medical or legal advice unless the test and report are specified for that use. For health-related questions, consult a qualified clinician.
Whether a legal or immigration report is accepted is decided by the requesting authority — an embassy, court or government department — and depends on their chain-of-custody and documentation requirements, not on us.
DNA Testing Solutions is not liable for any decisions made on the basis of your results.

Turnaround Time

Because your sample travels internationally, turnaround has two parts: courier transit from Lahore to the laboratory (normally 3–5 working days) and the laboratory's own processing time, which varies by test.
We confirm the expected timeframe in writing when you order. Delays may occur due to sample quality, courier handling, laboratory volume or other circumstances outside our control.
We do not quote a single turnaround for every test.

Refund Policy

Refunds may only be granted before your sample has been dispatched to the partner laboratory.
No refund will be issued once the sample has been shipped or laboratory processing has begun.
Shipping, handling and courier charges are non-refundable.
