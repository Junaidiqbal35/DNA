How a laboratory ends up on this page

Your report is only worth what the laboratory behind it is worth, so the choice is not made on price.

Accreditation we can see

We hold a copy of each laboratory's current certificate, and we publish it here. If a certificate lapses, the laboratory comes off this page until it is renewed.

Reports that get accepted

For legal and immigration work, what matters is whether the receiving authority accepts the laboratory's report. We send that work only to laboratories with a record of it being accepted.

A working relationship

We ship internationally every week, so we need a laboratory that answers questions about a shipment quickly. A lab we cannot reach is a lab we cannot use.

Whose accreditation this is

Every accreditation and certificate on this page belongs to the laboratory named beside it. DNA TESTING SOLUTIONS does not hold laboratory accreditation, does not claim any, and does not perform testing. Our Pakistan office coordinates, verifies, documents and ships samples; the laboratory tests them and issues the report.

These laboratories have no offices or testing facilities in Pakistan.
