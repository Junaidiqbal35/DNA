We are not a laboratory, and we say so

A lot of DNA services in Pakistan are vague about where testing actually happens. We would rather be plain about it. Our office does not contain testing equipment, because it does not need to.

What it contains is the part that usually goes wrong: identity checks, correct sealing, the right forms for the right laboratory, and a courier booking that gets a biological sample across borders without being delayed or rejected.

The division of work

Our Pakistan office is responsible for

Receiving samples from customers; verifying them against the customer and order details; preparing and packaging them for international shipment; completing the shipping and supporting documentation; dispatching through DHL or another international courier; tracking the shipment; coordinating with the laboratory; and supporting you throughout your order.

The partner laboratory is responsible for

DNA testing; genetic analysis; and the preparation and issuing of your results and report, under its own accreditation and quality system.

No DNA testing, extraction, genetic analysis, PCR or sequencing takes place at our Pakistan facility, and no results are prepared here. We do not provide genetic or medical diagnosis or treatment, and we do not operate as a pathology or diagnostic laboratory in Pakistan.

What we are actually good at

Everything between you handing over a swab and a laboratory on another continent opening a valid, correctly documented sample.

Getting the identity right

Every swab is matched to a named participant on your order and checked against identification before it is sealed. A sample that reaches the laboratory with the wrong name attached is worse than no sample at all.

Getting the paperwork right

Each laboratory, embassy and court wants its own forms, consents and identification. We complete them before dispatch, which is what stops an order stalling three weeks later.

Getting it there

Biological samples crossing borders need correct packaging, declarations and a tracked service. We book it, we track it, and we chase it if it stops moving.

Our laboratory partners

Samples go to established laboratories in the United Kingdom, the United States and Turkey. Which one handles your sample depends on the test and on who will receive the report.

Each laboratory's accreditation certificate is published in full, so you can read the credentials behind your report instead of taking our word for them.
