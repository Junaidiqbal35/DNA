Who needs to be tested

The people who must attend differ by test. Bringing the right participants and documents the first time is the single biggest thing that keeps an order on schedule.

Test Participants needed Sample Commonly used for
Paternity Child and alleged father. The mother's sample is optional but strengthens the result. Cheek swab Personal certainty; legal matters when ordered as a legal test
Maternity Child and alleged mother Cheek swab Personal certainty; records and documentation disputes
Siblingship Two or more siblings. A known parent's sample improves accuracy. Cheek swab Establishing a relationship when a parent is unavailable
Grandparent / aunt / uncle Child and the relative being tested Cheek swab Relationship questions where the parent cannot be tested
Immigration DNA Exactly the people named by the embassy or immigration authority Cheek swab Visa, residency and citizenship applications

Tests requiring a blood sample rather than a cheek swab are arranged separately through a clinical collection partner. Ask us before booking.

For immigration and court use, the authority receiving the report sets the rules on collection and identification. Tell us who the report is for before you book.

  • Identity and order verification
  • Sealing, labelling and paperwork

In transit

  • International courier to the laboratory
  • Airway bill shared with you

Extra participants, delivery of a collection kit to another city, and additional copies of a report are priced per order. Ask when you book and we will confirm the cost in writing.

Before you order

Still unsure which test you need? Call +92 307 456 1958.

Where is the test actually performed?
At a partner laboratory in the United Kingdom, the United States or Australia. Our Lahore office collects and ships the sample; it does not carry out testing, extraction or analysis. You are told which laboratory before dispatch.
What should I bring to the appointment?
Photo identification for every adult being tested, and the birth certificate or B-Form for any child. Legal and immigration tests have stricter identification rules, which we send you in your booking confirmation.
Can I collect the sample at home instead?
For personal-knowledge tests, we can discuss sending a collection kit. For legal and immigration tests, collection has to be witnessed and documented, so it must happen at our office or an approved location.
Does a cheek swab hurt?
No. A soft swab is rubbed inside the cheek for a few seconds. It is painless, needs no needles, and is safe for newborns and elderly participants alike.
What happens if the sample fails at the laboratory?
The laboratory tells us, and we arrange a fresh collection. Re-collection and re-shipping terms depend on the laboratory's policy for your test and are set out in your order confirmation.
