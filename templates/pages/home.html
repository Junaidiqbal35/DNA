Where your sample goes

6 stages
  1. Sample given Buccal (cheek) swab, taken in front of our staff
  2. Identity and order verified Swabs matched to the named participants on your file

What our Lahore office does, and what it doesn't

People often assume a DNA service in Pakistan runs its own laboratory. Ours does not, and we would rather you knew that before you book than after.

We do this, here in Lahore

  • Receive buccal swab samples from customers
  • Verify each sample against the customer and order details
  • Prepare and package samples for international shipment
  • Complete the shipping and supporting documentation
  • Dispatch by DHL or other international courier, and track the shipment
  • Coordinate with the laboratory and support you through your order

We do not do this in Pakistan

  • DNA testing or DNA extraction
  • Genetic analysis
  • PCR, sequencing or any other laboratory testing
  • Preparing DNA test results inside Pakistan
  • Genetic or medical diagnosis, or medical treatment
  • Operating as a pathology or diagnostic laboratory

So who does test it?

Established laboratories in the United Kingdom, the United States and Australia, each working under its own accreditation. We hold a copy of every current certificate and publish it, so you can check the credentials behind your report rather than take our word for them.

How an order runs, start to finish

Five stages. You are told which laboratory your sample is going to, and you can track it the whole way.

  1. 01

    Order and book

    Choose your test and book a time at the Lahore office. We confirm the price and who needs to attend.

  2. 02

    Give the swab

    A painless cheek swab, taken at our office. Bring the identity documents listed in your booking confirmation.

  3. 03

    Sealed and shipped

    We seal, label and document the samples, then hand them to DHL. You receive the tracking number.

  4. 04

    Tested abroad

    The partner laboratory in the UK, US or Australia carries out the testing and writes the report.

  5. 05

    Report to you

    Results are issued by the laboratory and sent to you. We stay available for anything to do with your order.

Why send samples abroad at all?

Because the report has to be trusted by whoever asked for it — a court, an embassy, a solicitor, or just the family involved. Sending the sample to an established laboratory abroad means the analysis and the report come from that laboratory, under its own accreditation and quality system.

One chain, documented

Collection, sealing, paperwork and dispatch are recorded at each step, in the format the receiving laboratory requires.

Tracked, not posted and hoped

Samples travel by international courier with an airway bill you can follow from Lahore to the laboratory's door.

The lab's own report

Results are prepared and issued by the laboratory that did the testing, on its own letterhead. We do not write or alter them.

Someone local to ask

You deal with an office in Lahore in your own language and time zone, rather than emailing another continent.

Questions people ask

Not covered here? Call +92 307 456 1958 or email info@dnalabpakistan.com.

Do you test the DNA yourselves?
No. Our Lahore office collects, verifies, packages and ships samples. All DNA extraction, analysis and reporting is done by the partner laboratory in the United Kingdom, the United States or Australia. We are not a pathology or diagnostic laboratory in Pakistan.
Which laboratory will handle my sample?
It depends on the test you order and the requirements of whoever asked for it. We tell you which laboratory and which country before your sample is dispatched, and the laboratory's details appear on your report.
How long does it take?
Courier transit from Lahore to the laboratory normally takes 3–5 working days. Processing time at the laboratory varies by test type and is confirmed in writing when you order. We do not quote a single turnaround for every test, because the sample has to travel internationally first.
Can I use the result for a visa, court case or inheritance matter?
Legal and immigration matters require a test taken under the receiving authority's chain-of-custody rules, and whether a report is accepted is decided by that authority — an embassy, court or government department — not by us. Tell us what the result is for before you book, so the correct test and documentation are arranged from the start.
Is my information kept private?
Your details and sample are shared only with the laboratory carrying out your test and the courier handling the shipment. We do not release results to anyone other than the people named on your order without your written instruction.
Will you explain my results?
We can explain the process, your paperwork and anything about the shipment. We cannot give genetic or medical interpretation, diagnosis or advice — those come from the issuing laboratory, and for health-related questions from a qualified clinician.
